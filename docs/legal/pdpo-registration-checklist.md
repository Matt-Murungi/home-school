# PDPO registration checklist (template, D06)

**Not legal advice; all fields and requirements are unverified until confirmed on the PDPO site (pdpo.go.ug) or by the lawyer.** Secondary sources (25 Nov 2026) say registration is online, uses "Form 2", is valid one year and renewed annually, may carry a fee (one guide: UGX 100,000, unconfirmed), and that cross-border transfers have a separate undertaking ("Form 3"). Confirm each. Consumed by C04 (#142). Launch blocker: track in R07 (#155).
