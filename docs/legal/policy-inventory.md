## Non-binding heading outlines (for the drafter, not legal text)

- **Terms for parents:** who we are; what the service is and is not (support alongside school); accounts and guardians; plans and payment; cancellation and refunds (link); adult present; conduct; liability; complaints; changes; governing law (lawyer).
- **Teacher agreement:** status; vetting and documents; assignments and offers; fees, tiers, payouts, tax (lawyer); code of conduct (link); confidentiality and data handling; incidents; suspension and termination; leaving a child's case.
- **Privacy notice:** controller; data collected per role; purposes and legal basis (lawyer); recipients and processors; hosting and transfers; retention; rights (view, export, delete); children; breach; contact and PDPO.
- **Safeguarding policy:** scope; principles; adult-present rule; reporting; case handling; roles; training; review.
- **Code of conduct:** home-visit behaviour; communication; photos; gifts; touch; alone-with-child rules; reporting duty.
- **Refund and cancellation:** cut-off; late cancellation; teacher cancellation and substitute; platform-caused misses; refund method.
- **Retention schedule:** table of data class, period, trigger, action, basis.
- **Cookie/permissions notice:** each permission, why, what if denied.
